// Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How do I access my support account?

Click the "Login to Support Portal" button above to access your existing support account. If you don't have an account, please contact us at info@symplistic.ai.

What information should I include in a support ticket?

Please include a detailed description of the issue, steps to reproduce the problem, any error messages, and your contact information. The more details you provide, the faster we can assist you.

What are your support hours?

Our standard support hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST. For emergency support, please call our direct line at (239) 319-5540.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We respond to support tickets based on severity. Severity 1 tickets (Critical) will be answered within 2-4 hours, Severity 2 tickets (High) will be answered within 4-8 hours, Severity 3 (Medium) will be answered within 1-2 business days and Severity 4 (Low) will be answered within 3-4 business days.

Can I track the status of my support ticket?

Yes, once you log into the support portal, you can view all your tickets, track their progress, and communicate with our support team directly.
